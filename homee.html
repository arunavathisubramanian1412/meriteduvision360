News: C-DAC Training Centre

Merit Arts and Science College is an Authorized Training Centre of C-DAC.

We are proud to announce that Merit Arts and Science College is an Authorized Training Centre of the Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC). C-DAC is a premier R&D organization of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, Government of India, recognized for its cutting-edge contributions in advanced computing, software technologies, and skill development. As an authorized training centre, our college offers students the opportunity to: Gain industry-relevant training under C-DAC’s approved curriculum. Access nationally recognized certification. Enhance their skills in emerging technologies and improve employability. This collaboration enables our students to stay ahead in today’s competitive IT and technology-driven world.

  • Industry-relevant training under C-DAC curriculum.
  • Nationally recognized certification.
  • Enhanced skills in emerging tech.

Welcome to Merit Arts and Science College

Chairman’s Message

At Merit Arts and Science College, our vision is deeply rooted in the belief that education is the most powerful tool for individual growth and societal progress. Since the inception of our institution, we have focused on creating a learning environment that empowers rural youth with academic excellence, moral strength, and life skills. We understand that in today’s competitive world, a degree alone is not enough. That’s why we ensure our students are equipped with values, technology, and global perspectives. It gives me immense pride to see our college evolve as a center of inclusive, value-based education that shapes responsible citizens. I urge every student to make the best use of the facilities, opportunities, and mentorship available on campus. Education is not just about learning facts, but about developing the ability to think independently, solve problems, and serve humanity. Let us walk together in this noble mission and continue building a future we can all be proud of. Welcome to the Merit family. – Mr. A. Subramanian, Chairman

– Mr. A. Subramanian, Chairman

Vice Chairman’s Message

As Vice Chairman, I feel privileged to be part of an institution that is committed to quality education, discipline, and character formation. At Merit Arts and Science College, we strive to provide a nurturing atmosphere where students can learn, grow, and discover their true potential. Our focus goes beyond academics — we believe in educating the heart and mind, ensuring our students evolve into well-rounded individuals. We offer an inclusive space for learning where every student, regardless of their background, is treated with dignity and given equal opportunity to succeed. It is our goal to equip our learners not only with subject knowledge but with values such as honesty, responsibility, and respect. I warmly invite parents, guardians, and aspiring students to join hands with us in this journey of transformation.
